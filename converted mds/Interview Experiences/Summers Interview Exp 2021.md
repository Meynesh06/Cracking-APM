1.  Were there any workex restrictions? Was relevant workex necessary?

PM Topics Covered: Product Design, Product Execution, Metrics, Behavioural, App Critique

2.  Were there any pre-processes (GD/case submissions/tests)? If yes details of those?
Yes, so there is a GD round before the interviews. Don’t worry, this is not a typical GD round
that most of us are scared of. (alteast it used to scare me off). It’s kind of a product design
round  only  and needs  to  be  done  individually.  In  the  common  teams  call,  there  will  be  an
announcement of the topic and then you will be sent to different breakout rooms and you will
be given 20 mins to write your product design document. At the end of the 20 mins, you need
to explain your approach to your moderator who will either be there throughout the call or
jumping between different breakout rooms. You also need to submit the word document you

prepared. Based on your submission and your explanation to the moderator shortlist for the
interviews will be given.

One tip from my side to crack this round - Try to cover each and every head under the CIRCLES
framework. Since the given time is 20 mins, do not spend much time on any one head. There
will be cross-questioning on the assumptions you made (Like for my solution I assumed that
the  product  will  be  first  launched  in  the  US  so  they  asked  why  US?  )  or  the  solution  you
provided so do not throw any jargon without having knowledge about it.

The topic for the GD: Design a new app or add functionality to any of the existing Microsoft
apps to make learning and/or teaching more enjoyable and productive for students and/or
teachers.

3.  Interview rounds and their details?
There were 2 rounds in total for me.
1st Round: Taken by Senior Product Manager - Middle Aged
  Asked  about  what  I  believe  is  the  product  manager’s  job.  I  explained  it  using  my  own
experience  as  a  head of  product  development  during  my  own start-up  in  my  UG.  Also,
connected it to my work-ex where also I got the opportunity to handle the E2E feature
launch. So always try to connect back these types of questions to real-life experiences of
yours. It establishes the credibility that you are not talking out of thin air that you have
empathy, you love to solve customer pain points, etc. but you have real experiences (be it
from your work ex or your UG projects or your own start-up) to back it up.

  After knowing that I had my own start-up in my final year he asked me to explain it. Be
thorough with all the nitty-gritty of the venture - the product design, value propositions,
GTM strategy, success metrics, revenue model, etc. For me, this only became a product
design question. Cross-questioned a lot about a lot of things but it was a fun and healthy
discussion instead of an interview.

  Asked  about  my  favourite  app,  I  explained  why  it  is  my  favourite  app  using  Utility,
Innovativeness and Ease of Use Framework. There were a lot of cross-questions, situation-
based questions and depth in the discussion. So whatever you choose, be very thorough
with the research of the app. Also, know about the industry that your favourite app is in. A
lot  of questions  about how  the  industry  is  doing and  the  industry’s  future.  So  research
about  the  competitors  and  industry  in  general  as  well.  You  will  be  asked  about
improvements, what do you not like about the app/want to remove.

2nd Round:
  Started with ‘introduce yourself’ and asked about why PM & why Microsoft initially.
  Then moved on to the question of “Favourite Microsoft product”. Again, I explained the
features of the app and why I like it. Asked about the improvements and a lot of situational
questions based on that. The interviewer was not quite responsive in this round, so it was
essential  to  maintain  your  composure & confidence  and  be  structured  in  whatever you
answer.

  Asked  if  I had  any  questions  for  him. Never  miss  this  shot  to  show your interest  in  the
company. Always prepare a question upfront before the interview. Ask about something
strategic in nature that has been launched in the recent past and also impacts India. I asked
about Azure Farmbeats and how Indian Agritech startups are leveraging Farmbeats in the

Indian context currently. The interviewer didn’t work on this product, so didn’t have any
knowledge, but he promised me to get back on this.

  Research about all the Microsoft products. They can ask about improving any Microsoft

  Be definitely ready with one Microsoft and one non-Microsoft favourite app with thorough

  Prepare product design questions on the apps designed for kids like a search engine for

  Prepare a well-researched question to ask at the end of the interview.

6.  General tips/suggestions?
Do a lot of product cases with your friends or like-minded people who are interested in PM
prep.  I  used  to  do  the  same  and  with  every  case  done  I  used  to  learn  something  new.  Be
thorough  with  the  frameworks  like  CIRCLES  for  product  design.  Be  ready  with  multiple
favourite apps with  proper  research.  Suppose you  are going  for  a Microsoft  interview  then
prepare one Microsoft and one non-Microsoft app.

Also, be ready with critiques of apps from each and every sector like fintech, ed-tech, food,
healthcare,  ride-hailing,  etc.  Concepts  like  Network  effects,  Economies  of  Scale,  etc  and
technologies  like  ML,  AR/VR,  Blockchain,  Cloud  should  be  well  understood  and  use  them
whenever you can while giving the solutions to a product design question. Be prepared with
your resume because product design questions can also be asked for the organisations you
already worked for.

Metrics are a very important part of any product design question. I suggest not to use any
framework here but instead make your custom metrics based on the question. Good metrics
suggested will fetch you brownie points. Overall, be structured in your approach.

Be in contact with your seniors and try to do some mock interviews with them as well. (Trust
me they are the best resource that you can have at IIM B)

In the end, keep smiling throughout the interview (It's a tried and tested strategy, fetched me
2 companies)

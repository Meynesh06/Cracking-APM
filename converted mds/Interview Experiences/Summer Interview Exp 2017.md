PM Topics Covered: Product Design, Metrics

Topic: How will you design a chess game for players

Step 1: First my consumers will be

1)  Beginners who have interest in chess and want to learn
2)  Players who have a minimum knowledge
3)  Tournament players and above

Step 2: In case of allocation of player, we will allocate player based on path dependency
for example if a player has won certain games with each game he will be getting some
points and based on the points all the players are categorized in to different levels and
opposite player will be allocated to a player based on the scores with in those levels. The
levels were categorized based on the number of wins for the game, any ratings like Chess
federation ratings and some key steps which are used during games which are used by
players like Carlson, Anand etc., If the player lost for more than 2 games then the player
to him will be allocated a level below to him so that his chances of winning will be high,
and  he  will  get  back  on  track  with  confidence.  Here  after  the  game  was  completed
recommended suggestions will be displayed as how Carlson would have played if he was
in the players position. How better the player can do the moves and where he can do and
analysis for his failure and success.

Step 3: Disadvantages or short comes

Here a player will be allocated other player based on his scores, but back end work will be
high  during  allocation  of  players.  There  will  be  lot  many  players  and  the  algorithms
processing will become difficult to get the right player as pair.

Step 4: To eliminate this

PRODUCT DESIGNING CASE

Design a product for smart home using the support of Microsoft Cortana. Also tell why Microsoft can have
an edge over Amazon and Google in the same.

Solution:

I started by dividing the users of smart home solutions based on age and identified some
sample use cases for each division:

1. Children-> Entertainment, Education

2.  Young adults-> Personal use: Entertainment, Communication, Monitoring Children
Professional use: Education, Office work

3. Senior citizens-> Entertainment, Emergency alarm

I focussed primarily on use case for Young Adults for the professional use as Microsoft is
a leader in professional and business applications.

I proposed a solution where Cortana can be used as a digital assistant to take transcripts
of a virtual meeting by converting the voice to text. Designed a wireframe and proposed
the metrics for measuring the performance.

INTERVIEW ROUND 1

Q1. How does Uber design its pricing strategy? Take a city of the size of Bangalore and
estimate.

For Base pricing, made some guestimates of the market size, the number of drivers and
average trip length, costs etc. And came up with a base pricing strategy.

5

Interviewer further extended the question: How do you determine surge?

I drew the basic Demand Supply curve where

Surge in a locality = Demand in terms of no. of users who are searching for a ride / Supply
in terms of no. of drivers ready to accept rides

E.g., if 1000 users are searching and 800 drivers are available, surge would be 1000/800 =
1.25

It was a very basic solution, but the interview was satisfied.

Q2. Suppose you are a Walmart store in a locality and are planning to sell car tyres. How
will you determine how many tyres to stock initially?

I proposed that to estimate the market size of tyres in the locality, we could look at the
economic division. Interviewer asked me to use data that is available.

So, I proposed that we could count the number of cars in the parking lot of Walmart to
estimate rough demand. Interviewer wasn't satisfied. So, I proposed that average value
of product in each customers basket can be measured. The customers purchasing higher
value of items in each bucket will be the ones most likely owning a car. Based on this data
we can estimate the market size.

Interviewer asked me to make a GTM strategy for the same.

I proposed that we could start by advertising in the store by giving free tyre check-ups to
customers  to  build  awareness.  Discount  coupons  can  be  provided  within  the  stores.
Referral and social media recommendation can also be used to acquire customers.

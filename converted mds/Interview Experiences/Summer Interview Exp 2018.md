BY ABHIMANYU SRIVASTAVA

PM Topics Covered: Product Design, Product Strategy, Root Cause Analysis

Describe the steps in the process i.e. no of interview rounds, GD, pre-process requirements
(LOI, case submission etc.).

Initially, 40-45 students were shortlisted based on resume for GD round. In the GD round,
they gave a case: How will you integrate LinkedIn with Microsoft Products. This round is
more individual-based, where you’ll be conversing with the interviewers on a regular basis
for almost 30 mins and will have to give a 1-minute pitch of your solution at the end. In
this round, the major focus is on structure and thinking pattern of the candidates. Please
note that you’ll have to submit the paper that you use to structure your thoughts, so make
sure that whatever you write is legible.

1.  Does work experience matter?

Though there were a few shortlists who didn’t have IT background, still there was some
weightage attached to it in terms of technical questions being asked in the later rounds.

2.  What kind of questions were asked? What is the general area of focus during the

interviews?

Round  1  (45  mins):  First  round  started  with  a  few  resume-based  questions,  the  focus
being on work experience mainly. I had worked on a project on Big Data, so they asked a
few questions regarding the project. After that, I was asked how I will identify Java related
keywords given a piece of text. The focus was on Machine Learning in this question and
how will you identify training set in this.

After that I was asked that if a B2B product is going to be late, then how you will identify
the  features  to  be  put  first.  Be  ready  to  take  a  decision  based  on  various  metrics  and
customer requirements.

Finally, I was asked why you want to join Microsoft and then they discussed about Kaizala
app. So, be sure to know enough details about Microsoft products.

Round 2: “You’re a PM for data-centre operations which is being used by various start-
ups. There is a natural calamity and the data-centre can’t operate. What will you do in this
situation? What will you do if there’s no backup for the last few days?”

Select  any  Microsoft  product,  identify  a  problem  in  it  and  suggest  ways  to  resolve  the
problem. Make sure that the problem that you are trying to solve in this round is not very
small.

Round  3:  How  will  you  design  a  tech-based  product  for  kids  so  that  they  are  used  to
Microsoft products and will keep on using it when they grow up? The product could have
been based on an existing Microsoft product/technology or an entirely new product. In
this question, I had to identify the stakeholders – kids (what age group), parents, teachers
etc. who will be influencing the use of product. Based on their needs I had to come up
with a set of features to be offered and then focus on technology which can enable these
features. Further we also discussed monetization of the product.

3.  What kind of HR questions were asked?

Why Microsoft?
Those who didn’t have relevant work experience or internship were also asked why they
wanted to go for PM role.

4.  How do you recommend a PGP2 should structure his preparation? Resources/ tools that

can help aid in preparation?

  Their GD round and case problems are focused on Microsoft products, so make

sure that you’ve at least some idea about all the major products.

  You  should  know  how  to  solve  a  product-design  questions.  Practice  them

thoroughly before the interviews.

  Be thorough with latest technologies. If you mention any of them in the interview,
the  interviewers  will  expect  you  to  know  about  it  thoroughly.  It  also  gives  you
some edge over others.

First Round: Case GD (for all the shortlisted candidates) – They gave a case: How will you
integrate  LinkedIn  with  Microsoft  Products.  You  will  get  30  min  for  solving  this.  In
between the interviewers will come to you and ask about your approach. After 30 min
everyone must give a 1 min pitch of their solution. If you give a structured pitch (briefing
all  the  steps  you  considered  for  solving  this),  I  think  your  chances  of  getting  selected
increase.

After this I had 3 rounds of interviews involving technical questions, product questions,
Guesstimates, general CV questions and questions related to latest tech news.

1.  Does work experience matter?

Yes, I think in laterals, work experience matters. But even if you don’t have IT background,

2.  What kind of questions were asked? What is the general area of focus during the

interviews?

Round 1: I was asked to give ideas for monetization of WhatsApp. I suggested some ideas,
and then he asked me to do a guesstimate of the revenue which will be generated through
my ideas. Then I was asked to do a cost-benefit analysis of server space utilized to run
WhatsApp vs revenue generated through my guesstimate.

He also asked me how peer to peer messaging works (this question is only relevant for
those who have IT background). He was asking about Client Server and P2P model. So, be
prepared to get 1 or 2 questions like this.

Round 2: How will you detect fake news on Facebook? Give your approach and suggest
ideas to prevent it. Then I was asked to explain the economics behind surge  pricing  of
Uber and derive formulae for it. Please read Microeconomics (Demand and Supply curve)
or study solution for this problem on the internet. The surge pricing is a standard problem
asked everywhere in PM interviews.

Round 3: Why did Ola acquire Foodpanda? In this I followed an approach given in case
book written by Issac Jojey (for consult interviews). Please refer to M&A section of the
casebook.

3.  What kind of HR questions were asked?

Why PM?
Why Microsoft?
Why you did internship in Consulting (if you didn’t do intern in PM role, you should
prepare for this question)

4.  How do you recommend a PGP2 should structure his preparation? Resources/ tools that

can help aid in preparation?

  First of all, you should read Crack the Interview for Program managers. This book
is very comprehensive. So, you should pick and choose what to study and what not
to study

  Practice Product questions (Design a product, favourite product, product pricing

models, e-commerce revenue models)

  Guesstimates
  Read Case interviews by Issac Jojey (this is helpful in structuring your thoughts,

may not be directly applicable for PM interviews)

  Be in touch with latest Tech events


If you have time revise DS1, DS2, Marketing Management and Microeconomics

Product Management

BY ABHIMANYU SRIVASTAVA

PM Topics Covered: Product Strategy, App Critique, Behavioral

Describe the steps in the process i.e. no of interview rounds, GD, pre-process requirements
(LOI, case submission etc.).

All the applicants were provided a case and had to submit a presentation for the same.
After that, around 25 students were shortlisted for interviews. The topic of the case  was
– “Choose a product or product feature that you use or love and then suggest ways to
improve it. How would you measure and know if the product or feature has improved?”

Once shortlisted after this, I went through 5 rounds of interviews which focused on case-
solving, product-strategy questions, technical questions and finally a few resume-based
and HR questions.

1.  Does work experience matter?

I think work ex plays a small part in initial shortlist. But since they have a technical round,
it's good to have tech knowledge. I mean you won't be able to crack it if you haven't done
any coding in life.

2.  What kind of questions were asked? What is the general area of focus during the

interviews?

Round 1 (30-45 mins): First round started with tell me about yourself followed by a case-
based question – The site-wide conversion for EBay India has decreased lately. What might
be  the  reasons?  On  approaching  the  problem  through  customer  purchase  funnel,  I
identified the problem in payment gateway. Then they said that the payment gateway is
provided by  a 3rd  party and  asked  for  a  resolution that  can  be  implemented  in  a  short
period of time.

Round 2 (1 hour): This round focused on product strategy questions in which I was asked
to develop a marketplace for refurbished or second-hand products. The discussion was
focused on identifying the product, market (B2B, B2C or C2C) and associated problems
with  each  of  these  (seller  verification  etc.),  pricing  strategy,  features  to  be  present,
whether it should be part of Flipkart or a separate website, and finally look and feel of the
website. My advice would be to start by looking at problems that customer will face while
buying such a product and how they can be solved.

Round 3 (1 hour): Initial discussion in this round was on – “What is your favourite app?
What  do  you  like  the  most  about  it?  What  metrics  would  you  use  to  track  its
performance?”

After  that,  they  gave  another  site-wide  conversion  issue.  Using  the  same  customer
purchase funnel’ approach, the problem was identified to be dropping of customers after
search.  The  reason  was  identified  to  be  change  in  product  types  due  to  old  vendors
moving  out  and  new  vendors  coming  in.  Finally  they  asked  to  suggest  a  few  ways  to
resolve the issue.

Round 4 (1 hour): This round mainly focused on technical questions. I was asked to select
an app and explain how to design it with exact features. Then they asked how to divide
the  work  in  a  team  of  4/5  people.  Apart  from  that,  they  also  asked  about  frontend-
backend integration for login and verification, and how relational databases can be used.
Since I am not from a Computer Science background, the interviewer helped a little bit
here by telling that the work should be divided in frontend and backend work.

Round 5 (45 mins): The round started with resume-based questions and a few generic HR
questions like ‘Tell me about yourself’ etc. After that they asked to suggest a category that
Flipkart should enter. I jumped into the solution directly, but they wanted me to take a
structured  approach,  starting  with  their  capabilities  and  the  right  market  for  their
capabilities, analysing them, identify a solution and then find a few challenges that you
might face.

3.  What kind of HR questions were asked?
Tell me about yourself, Why Flipkart

4.  How do you recommend a PGP2 should structure his preparation? Resources/ tools that

can help aid in preparation?

  Consulting case preparation will help a lot to effectively approach the case-solving

questions.

  Flipkart is obsessed with customers, so always keep them in mind before

answering any question.

  Read about company and its products thoroughly and you should be aware of

latest happenings in the company.

BY CHANDRIMA PAUL

PM Topics Covered: Behavioral

Describe the steps in the process i.e. no of interview rounds, GD, pre-process requirements
(LOI, case submission etc).

The company came for 2 roles – Tech Strategy (TST), and Strategy & Operations. While for
S&O, there were video interviews with 3-4 questions (time limit – 2 mins each), for TST
role, candidates were directly shortlisted based on CV. There was no GD for either of the
roles.
Around 30-35 students were shortlisted for TST, who were assigned a buddy each. The
shortlisting and assigning of buddy were done around 4 days before the interview. The
buddies  helped  with  the  preparation  material  and  provided  guidance  for  tech-based
cases.  A day  before the interviews, Deloitte also  conducted a workshop for tech-based
cases.  The  workshop  was  interactive,  and  everyone  was  provided  a  sheet  with  their
names.  As  the  people  present  in  the  workshop  were  also  there  during  the  interviews,
participation in the workshop also played a role in the selection process. In the end, the
company selected 5 people from the campus for TST role.

1.  Does work experience matter?

All the students shortlisted for TST role had a tech background.

2.  What  kind  of  questions  were  asked?  What  is  the  general  area  of  focus  during  the

interviews?
a.  Round  1:  Interview  round  1  was  mainly  focused  on  introduction  and  CV-based
questions.  Most  of  the  students  were  also  asked  to  solve  guesstimates,  but  not
everyone. This round was also an elimination round, but only a few were eliminated.
b.  Round 2: This round was a major elimination round and had a tech-based case. In my
case, the case was related to IT M&A. The approach to solve tech-based cases was also
informed in the workshop held and it really helped in this round. I was directly selected
after the round, but others went through a 3rd round of interview too.

c.  Round  3:  This  was  a  partner  round  and  mainly  focused  on  HR  questions  only.  The
objective of this round was to check the cultural fit of interviewees with the company.
The  elimination  was  there  only  if  the  round  went  very  bad,  otherwise  most  were
selected.

3.  What kind of HR questions were asked?

To test the cultural fit of interviewees with the company.

4.  How do you recommend a pgp2 should structure his preparation? Resources/ tools that

can help aid in preparation?
You should be thorough with what’s written in your resumes and should practice solving
guesstimates  as  it  was  there  for  most  in  the  first  round.  For  tech-based  cases,  both  –
material (frameworks and approach) and guidance, were provided by the assigned buddy.
But the buddies were assigned only 4 days before the interviews, so preparing for such

cases in advance will be helpful. A workshop was also conducted by the company a day
before the interviews which was focused on solving tech-based cases only.

Introduction
Groww aims to simplify complex financial products in India,
especially for first-time and young investors. The Groww app
allows users to invest in fixed deposits, stocks, gold, mutual funds
and develop a savings portfolio.:

The company is currently looking to foray into the neobanking
space with a new neobanking platform that it will likely launch
soon. According to one of the sources close to Groww, the
company believes that being a neo-banking company will further
make it holistic for the users, which want to emerge as a one-stop
solution for banking and investment.

User Personas
Beginner

Are new to the world of Finance. Hardly know anything about
investments/trading or any related terms.
Wealth creation in the long run. Fears of losing money due to
lack of guidance on building portfolio.

Intermediate Trader

They have explored / self-learned about Finance. They are not
completely new nor have expertise.
They have multiple options to choose from and are not able to
understand technical terms involved in the final decision like
Tracking Error, Expense Ratio, Exit Load & Taxation.

Expert Trader

They have been in the world of Markets for a long tenure. They
don't seek/seek for very less guidance.
They want to do trading on a smooth Platform that is glitch-free

Functionalities - Analysis

1) Mutual Funds Portfolio Analysis

Interesting Features

Portfolio analysis on Groww is super user-friendly and
compact. Visual representations of investment analysis,
category-split, and company-split provide investor with
the tools to remain in control of their investments.

2) Fund Transactions

Interesting Features

This feature delivers transparency in form of a neatly

compiled audit trail which is an easy stop for the investor

interested in transactions and NAV history.

3) Switch
Interesting Features
Groww’s fund switch. The switch enables the investor to shift
from one fund to another which is managed by the same
Asset Management Company. Right now, the user has to
redeem all the assets while switching which can also attract
exit loads.

Recommendations

Charts analysis sharing – Build a feature to share
the chart analysis done on single or multiple
instruments with other Kite users within the app and
feature to download it as an image/pdf which can be
shared via other mediums
Forum for traders – Build a forum for traders to
discuss strategies, experiences, opinions, grievances,
and connect with like-minded people. This can be
made free to use for Kite users
Debt Easy finder - Allow you to search debt using any
or all combinations of name, maturity date, and
coupon code instead of the ISN code. This will list all
the debt available in a convenient manner on basis of
the user filter
Watchlist Filter - Allows you to filter the stocks in the
watchlist on basis of other parameters such as market
cap  - small, mid, and large-capitalization and/or sector
composition - tech, FMCG, pharma, etc
Stock Tagging - Allows you to add a custom tag on
stocks which can be used for quick filtering and
identification of special stocks at multiple places
Planned Announcements- News concerning the
company's internal announcements like bonuses,
dividends, split, rights issues, etc should be marked on
the stock chart to explain planned sudden price
movements of shares and correlate them on the chart

Recommendations

Virtual Investment Leader board: Users get Virtual
Money on a Monthly Basis to trade on a simulation-based
platform that follows real-time market movement, this
helps them build confidence in their learning from Groww
App and Groww Pro. A Leader board would highlight the
top performers over the month in Equity, Futures and
Options, and Commodities.
Crypto Learning and Simulation: As per Analysts it's
very difficult for Indian brokers like Groww and Zerodha to
get into Crypto Trading as those are completely
unregulated markets. Groww can launch its own Crypto
Learning and Simulation Feature. If done so, it has the
potential to attract young Gen Z Investors who are finding
the right guidance to learn Crypto. This would be a Paid
Add-On Web Feature.

Metrics to track

North Star: Number of Daily Active Users (DAUs)
Acquisition

Total number of new app downloads per week.
Rate of people in the middle of sign-up process per week

Activation

% of users who have completed the registration process to
new app downloads in a week
Average amount of funds per user transferred in the wallet

Revenue

Revenue earned from brokerage per day per trading category
Average brokerage earned per user per week

Retention

Number of Daily Active Users/Monthly Active Users
User churn rate - users who are not trading for 6 months ÷
users present at the beginning of the 6 month period

Referral

% of total users who signed up via invite links per week
Total invite links shared externally per week

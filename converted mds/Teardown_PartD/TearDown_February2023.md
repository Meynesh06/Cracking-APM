Introduction

Sportskeeda is a global sports and esports news website founded in 2009. It is
known to be the go-to platform for hardcore sports fans. Its product offerings
include  exclusive  content,  live  scores,  videos,  and  in-depth  articles  produced
by experienced sports journalists. Nazara Technologies acquired a 67% stake
in Sportskeeda in 2019 for Rs. 44 Crores.

Insights

Cricket Related Content is Booming
The  most  popular  sport  in  India,  cricket,  is  by  far  the  most  searched  sport
online. Cricket queries have grown by 80% in just the last two years.

Online interest in football is extending beyond premium audiences
The advent of leagues such as ISL has led to diversifying sports interests. While
the  English  Premier  League,  Spanish  La  Liga,  and  Italian  Series  A  find  their
largest traction in metro cities, ISL finds its largest interest in Tier 2 Cities.

 An Olympic medal drives newfound interest in badminton
Badminton is the most hero-led sport in India, with sustained search interest
in  stars  such  as  Sindhu  and  Nehwal.  This  provides  opportunities  to  build  a
strong association with fans and reach out to the audience innovatively.

Kabaddi is beginning to build an audience online.
As  kabaddi  evolves  and  strengthens  roots  across  all  audience  categories,  it
opens a new arena to target sports fans.

Competitors

User Personas
The Digital Native

Active  on  Social  Media  use  mobile  to  consume  bite-sized  content  for
scores and updates.

The Micro-Influencer

Influences  the  group  on  the  content  to  be  watched.  Organizes  watch
parties. Attends games with the premium seating arrangement. Infrequent
in watching games.

Neo-Classical

They are the modernized version of old super fans. Watch matches on TV.
Crave behind the scene content and access to favorite players and teams.

Functionalities

Predictions & Rewards

Pain Points
The content consumers lack active participation leading to lower engagement.

Solution
• Voting and Prediction popups, open hours before the match begins
• Coins won as a reward can be redeemed on other apps in the form of
offers.

Sports Merchandising And Bidding

Pain Points
•  Unable  to  buy  sports  merchandise  for  the  team  or  player  they  feel
attached to
• Not being rewarded for loyalty and hence do not feel affiliated.

Solution
• Partnering with 3rd Party vendors to sell the merchandise
•  Auction  of  exclusive.  Original  editions  of  merchandise  on  the  app.
Bidding is to be hosted by Sportskeeda for a limited time.

Community Pages

Pain Points
•  Users  are  unable  to  interact  with  similar  fans  via  the  platform.  They
are  interested  in  watch  parties  and  would  want  to  use  a  platform  for
the same

Solution
• Player and team-specific community pages
•  The  sports  fans  will  be  able  to  chat,  comment  &  share  pictures  with
people of similar interest
•  Location-specific  watch  parties  can  be  organized,  with  live  screening
partnerships with pubs and restaurants.

Monetization Strategy

Sportskeeda’s  revenues  are  mainly  advertising,  and  sponsorship  is
driven.  However,  Nazara  Technologies  had  decided  to  add  premium
and subscription sections to open up more revenue streams.

•  Since  the  onset  of  Covid,  due  to  sports  events  cancellations,
Sportskeeda  has  jumped  to  Esports  and  Gaming,  quadrupling  the
revenue from April 2020.
•  Wrestling  section  seems  to  be  the  biggest  revenue  driver  in  a  Covid
scenario

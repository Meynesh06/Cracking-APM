Introduction

CRED  is  an  Indian  Unicorn  with  a  current  user  base  of  11.2  Million.  It  is  a
distinctive membership-based platform redefining credit card management
in  India.  By  facilitating  effortless  bill  payments,  providing  rewards  for
punctuality, and unlocking exclusive deals, CRED transforms the way users
interact with their credit cards.

Over time, CRED has grown to become a more holistic solution with added
features.  It  now  allow  users  to  make  peer-to-peer  credit  transactions,  pay
rent,  make  merchant  payments,  manage  their  credit  score,  and  invest
through  the  a  now  allow  users  to  make  peer-to-peer  credit  transactions,

The platform's  emphasis on  security ensures a  protected environment for
users,  empowering  them  to  make  informed  financial  decisions  while
enjoying  the  benefits  of  convenience,  rewards,  and  a  thriving  financial
community.

Functionalities
Credit Card Bill Payment

Interesting Features

One-click solution for credit card bill payment.
Secure payments can be made using PIN, fingerprints, and phone patterns.
A reminder for bill payment is sent as a push notification with the pending
amount.
Provides complete information on hidden charges.

Pain Points

In  case  there  are  no  expenses  on  the  card  for  the  month,  a
notification is still sent to check if the bills are pending.

Solution

The  users  should  be  sent  a  push  notification  about  zero  expenses
for the month, instead of asking them to check for the bill.

Wholistic Dashboard

Interesting Features

One-stop monitor for all your credit needs.
Allows  you  to  monitor  your  credit  card,  rewards  earned,  and  spent
on lifestyle within the app.
Alert users of any hidden charges in their credit card statements.

Pain Points

CRED  scan  score  is  not  shown  on  the  dashboard  which  might  be
important for the users to track rewards/payment.

Solution

The  users  should  be  able  to  see  their  spends/payments  through
CRED to reward ratio this will instigate a habit of timely payments.

Know Your Credit Score

Interesting Features

Easy access to credit score.
Categorise  credit  score  as  healthy  or  unhealthy  through  color
coding.
Easy to detect a reduction in credit score.
Also provides access to Experian and CRIF scores.

Paint Points
There is no means to generate a statement to report disputes

Solution
•  Allowing  users  to  generate  a  credit  report  within  the  app  and  using
the same to report a credit issue.

Metrics to Track

North Star: Number of Transactions per month

L1 Metrics: Recurring transactions peruse.
L2 Metrics: Average transaction value.

Active Users:  This  metric  measures  the  number  of  users  who  actively
engage  with  the  CRED  app  within  a  specific  time  frame,  such  as  daily
and monthly active.

User  Retention:  User  retention  measures  the  percentage  of  users
who continue to use the CRED app over a specific period

Conversion  Rate:  The  conversion  rate  measures  the  percentage  of
users  who  take  a  desired  action  within  the  app,  such  as  making  credit
card bill payments or engaging with partner offers.

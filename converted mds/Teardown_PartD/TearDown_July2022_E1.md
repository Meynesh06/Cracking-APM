Introduction
Spotify is a Swedish firm which is known for its music streaming
application, available in ios/android/windows platforms. It has
acquired 24 organizations till date. It offers a subscription service
to over 82 million copyrighted music & podcasts. The current
user base stands at 182 million subscribers from 180+ countries.
Spotify offers two types of subscription -
1.Spotify Free - Ad included, limited music and quality with no
download capability
2.Spotify Premium - Ad free, feature-unlocked & 320kbps
quality music with download option
Unlike Youtube, artists do not receive royalties on spotify based
on the number of listeners but instead they receive royalties are
given to artists vary based on how their music is streamed by
users or the agreements signed.
The different genre available are -
1.Educational 6. Music
2.Comedy 7. TV
3.News & Politics 8. Lifestyle & Health
4.Stories
5.Sports

3) Music Curration
Interesting Features
Collaborative Playlist: Create shared playlist with friends by
simply changing the playlist to a collaborative playlist useful for
trips and parties
Radio: This feature creates a radio of songs similar to your
selection, genre, artist. Useful in scenarios where one can play
similar songs without searching
Weekly Discovery, Daily Mixes: Discovery curates new songs
similar to taste every monday. Daily Mixes is a similar version
with daily new songs to enjoy based on its recommendation
Pain Points
When we forget a song and remember the tune alone it's
almost difficult to find via Spotify even with such good
recommendation engine
Identifying friends with similar music taste is difficult in Spotify,
ability to view a music profile will boost engagement and
interaction

Solutions
A personalized program for beginner artists –
curated mentorship program and provide tools to
budding artist to create their first few songs, provide
them with required training, resources and
professional help to create albums / podcast that help
talent go further.
Spotify music profiles: gives an archive and
snapshot of a person’s musical 'life' on spotify . The
date that you first listened to a song on spotify,
playcount of your favourite tracks, 'newly discovered' -
the songs you've recently listened to for the first time
then listened to lots since, 'mutual songs'
Music recognition- Allow you to recognize the music
just by playing or singing the song. You can see the
name of the music, artist and even add the music to
your own playlist.
Social Playlist - Allows you to share a playlist with
your friends and family members so that everyone can
add their favourite song for any social occasion such
as parties, family functions etc.
Custom Music Tagging - Allows you to add a custom
tag on music which can be used for quick filtering and
identification of music at multiple playlists or search
tabs.
New Music- Discover new music by artists who the
user already listens to or is very similar to the existing
music taste of the user. The song should have a new
tag to indicate that it is a latest release.

Metrics to track
North Star: Number of Daily Active Users (DAUs)
Acquisition
Total number of new app downloads per week.
Rate of people in the middle of sign-up process of spotify
premium per week
Activation
% of users who have completed the registration process to
new app downloads in a week and listened to a song
Total number of spotify premium users signed up in a week
Revenue
Revenue earned from spotify premium per day
Total number of ads watched by the users per day
Retention
Number of Daily Active Users/Monthly Active Users
User churn rate - users who are not listening to music for 6
months/users present at the beginning of the 6 month period
Referral
% of total users who signed up via invite links per week
Total invite links shared externally per week

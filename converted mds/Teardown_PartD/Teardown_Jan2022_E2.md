Introduction

Instagram is a photo and video sharing social networking
platform owned by Meta which can be used both on
mobile applications and web browsers.

Originally launched for iOS in October 2010, it rapidly
gained popularity, with one million registered users in two
months, 10 million in a year, and 1 billion as of June 2018.

 It offers various functionalities such as chat function, video
calls, stories, reels and much more.

Instagram is ranked as the 4th most popular social
network by number of users.

3) Messaging and Calls

Interesting Features

Watch Together - When on a video call with their loved
ones or colleagues, users can watch their favorite
posts, reels, or accounts together
Vanish Mode – This protects user's privacy when they
want to talk about something confidential,
automatically erasing the chat after the mode is turned
off
Hidden Requests - Requests of messages from
suspicious accounts are automatically sent here, letting
users stay untroubled by mysterious activities
Accept/Decline Message Requests - Gives users the
option of accepting or declining message request from
unknown accounts without letting the other person
know about the read status

Pain Points

Search Chat - Long-lost chats or important messages

cannot be found by just typing the search keywords

Mark Messages – Users cannot mark or highlight

important messages and threads in the chat

Media Categorization – Shared posts, reels,

documents, images, etc. are not available in

categorized manner in chat

Swipe Reply for Reels – Reply to reels is possible only

through long press; swiping for quick reply is not

available

Sticker Sharing – Users cannot share their favorite

stickers or sticker packs with one another

Solutions

Reels Sharing – Enable the option to send the reel as
DM to an individual or in a group chat and then
further allowing the same reel to be shared from the
chat on the profile. This way, it will get easier to get it a
review before an individual posts it
Chat Window - Add a search feature to search for
specific messages in the chat. Add a section of stickers
to send along with a sticker customization feature for
users to create their own stickers. Allow swipe reply
for reels sent/received in DMs

Reels Collection - While reels collection can be made
and managed from the saved folder, there is lack of an
option of saving it to a collection directly while saving
the reel itself
Media Manager - Categorize the media sent in chats
into different sections like reels, pictures/videos, audio
messages, etc. which will save time and efforts of user
Search Products - Build a feature for the Instagram
shop that allows users to search for specific products
on Instagram and allow businesses' added
hashtags/description to be searched. Hashtags and
description will help narrow down the search for the
user and promote trending products as well
Hashtag Suggestions - Suggest hashtags to
businesses intelligently by identifying the
colors/objects in the image of their product and also
suggest hashtags based on trends
Reviews - Enable reviews for products listed by
different businesses. This will organize reviews that are
currently posted by buyers as comments as they will
be able to review the product directly on the product's
page
Product Catalogue - Build a product catalogue by
using the information provided by the businesses with
smart categorization and trends comprised in the
home page along with search/suggestions of products
based on user preferences like the explore page
Star messages - Develop a feature to allow users to
mark/highlight specific messages that they wish to
save and come back to later easily

Smart Promoter – Use AI/ML to assist
users/businesses by suggesting them influencers or
pages that they can do a paid promotion of their
products with. They can search for accounts to partner
using filters like influencer reach, budget, location, etc.

Metrics to track
North star metric - Number of Daily Active
Users (DAUs)

Acquisition

Total number of new users signing up on instagram
per week
Conversion rate from landing on app store/play store
to app download for new users

Activation

Total number of new users spending 15 minutes or
more on the app per week
Avg. time taken for new users to complete their profile

Revenue

Total revenue earned from ads impressions per week
Total revenue earned from ads clicks per week
Conversion rate from total impressions to total
number of purchases via Instagram shopping

Retention

Number of Daily Active Users/Monthly Active Users
User churn rate - users who left in a month ÷ users at
the beginning of the particular month

Referral

% of total users who signed up via invite links per
week
Total Instagram invite links shared externally per
week

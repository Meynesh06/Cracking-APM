Introduction
Zerodha is a discount brokerage platform with a very clean UI
and is easy to learn because of its elegant design. It has multiple
applications under its umbrella:

Kite - Flagship Trading platform
Console - Central Dashboard cum Backoffice platform
Coin - Direct mutual fund platform
Varsity - Education App to increase financial literacy.

Zerodha is a combination of Zero and Rodha which in Sanskrit
means barrier. Launched on 15th August 2010, it now has 8
million clients and contributes to 15% of all Indian retail trading
volumes. It charges Rs 0 brokerage for equity delivery trades and
direct mutual funds and is one of the most transparent brokers

Discount Broker - Carries out buy and sell orders at
reduced commission rates, but does not provide investment
advice or perform analysis on a client's behalf
Full-Service Broker - Provide a range of services to its
customers, have branch offices and dedicated relationship
managers, but charge higher brokerage

Functionalities - Analysis
1) Trading

Interesting Features

Good Till triggered (GTT) - Allows you to set conditions
that remain valid across multiple sessions.
Customizable Alerts - User-defined settings that push
notification when the share hits the trigger condition.
Intra Day Separation - Any open positions are shown
separately under the "Positions" tab for easy tracking.
Gifting - You can simply transfer ownership of shares to
any Zerodha account with a nominal fee.
Margin Availability - Before actual trading of shares, one
directly knows the amount of margin required for trade
Baskets - Allows you to save up to 20 orders in a single
basket for the future and trade at the same time.

3) Watchlists

Interesting Features

Categorization: Can create up to 7 different stock lists consisting

of a maximum of 50 different stocks to categorize the different

stocks of interest and track their movements

Rearrangement: The order of stocks defined in the watchlist

determines the order in which they are shown in the tracking

dashboard. You can go ahead and rearrange them if required

Pain Points

No easy way to filter the watchlist on other parameters like
market capitalization - small, mid, and large-cap or by sectors -
tech, pharma, FMCG, etc

Solutions

Charts analysis sharing – Build a feature to share
the chart analysis done on single or multiple
instruments with other Kite users within the app and
feature to download it as an image/pdf which can be
shared via other mediums
Forum for traders – Build a forum for traders to
discuss strategies, experiences, opinions, grievances,
and connect with like-minded people. This can be
made free to use for Kite users
Debt Easy finder - Allow you to search debt using any
or all combinations of name, maturity date, and
coupon code instead of the ISN code. This will list all
the debt available in a convenient manner on basis of
the user filter
Watchlist Filter - Allows you to filter the stocks in the
watchlist on basis of other parameters such as market
cap  - small, mid, and large-capitalization and/or sector
composition - tech, FMCG, pharma, etc
Stock Tagging - Allows you to add a custom tag on
stocks which can be used for quick filtering and
identification of special stocks at multiple places
Planned Announcements- News concerning the
company's internal announcements like bonuses,
dividends, split, rights issues, etc should be marked on
the stock chart to explain planned sudden price
movements of shares and correlate them on the chart.

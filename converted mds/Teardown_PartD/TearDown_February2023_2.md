Introduction
Blinkit, formerly known as Grofers, is an online grocery delivery
service based out of Gurgaon, India. It currently operates in 500+
localities in more than 30 cities in India and offers 13000 unique
products. It is a quick commerce company that promises to
deliver groceries in minutes - in the blink of an eye. It currently
has 400 dark stores and is looking to expand this number by 40%
over the next 12 months. In 2022, Blinkit was acquired by Zomato
in an all-stock deal for USD 568 million.
Competitors

Growth of Blinkit
Grofers was founded in 2013 by Saurabh Kumar and Albinder
Dhindsa. It was one of the early movers in the online groceries
delivery space with sizeable market share. Grofers became a
unicorn in June 2021 with investments Zomato and Tiger Global.
After facing stiff competition from Zepto, Grofers pivoted to quick
commerce in Dec 2021. Grofers rebranded to Blinkit and shut
down operations in cities where they could not fulfil 10 minute
deliveries. This led to cash crunch for blinkit and Zomato extended
a loan of $150 million. In June 2022, Zomato's board approved the
acquisition of Blinkit in an all stock deal.
User Personas
The Planned shopper
Urban, tech-savvy user who shops mostly via digital channels
Has a busy schedule and prefers the convenience of online
shopping
Likes to get groceries delivered home on a weekly basis
The Spontaneous shopper
Young, tech-savvy user who forgets to buy groceries
Likes to makes spontaneous purchases such as chocolates and
experiment with ingredients
Has no regular schedule for grocery delivery
The Discount hunter
Value oriented customer who wants the best price for purchase
Spends time searching across platforms for the best deals and
offers

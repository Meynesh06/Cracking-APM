User Personas
Novice User

Characteristics : Occasional Exerciser, Inexperienced
Goals : Takes small steps to lose or maintain weight and stay fit
Pain Points : Dislikes exercise, Needs reminders to eat
healthier, Can't keep track of progress

Health User

Characteristics : Health issues, compelled to be fit
Goals : Control diet and habits to solve health issues. Build
awareness of the effects of daily habits
Pain Points : Difficulty keeping all metrics and data in one
platform, Obtaining vital information to provide to doctor

Fitness Enthusiast

Characteristics : Frequent Gym goer, Tech Savvy
Goals : Improve workout quality and intensity over time for
professional training
Pain Points : Trouble fitting work-out times between busy
schedules, Unsure if they are making actual gains

Functionalities - Analysis
1)Cure.fit

Interesting Features

Energy Meter - Live tracking of hand and leg movements
to check co-ordination with the trainer and his/her
steps. Based on the similarity score, a score and rank is
given to each user that is indicative of their energy
levels during the workout
Fitness Journey -Summary of the number of classes
attended per workout style and the calories burnt in
the process. It also shows the duration of workout and
visualizes monthly progress in a graphical format
Trending Sessions and Collections - Based on the most
popular workout formats, this feature helps users
choose the most trending ones. Also, collections based
on type of workout and intensity have been segregated
for enabling easy decision-making

Pain Points

Energy meter points difficult to track on the screen
during workout and user cannot infer anything in real
time during workout about his/her energy levels
Fitness journey gives information across classes, it does
not show any useful inference during the journey in a
workout and is challenging to improve upon the vague
metrics
Huge collection of workout makes it difficult to choose
the most appropriate one and sometimes when user
doesn't know where to start, such huge amount of
information can be overwhelming

2) Cult.fit store

Interesting Features

Designer's Note - The product details page contains trivia
about the product which gives a unique selling point to
each product in a more readable format
Shop by Workout - Products have been categorised by
workout type which makes it easier for users, especially
amateurs to find the right sports gear and accessories.
The app also shows collections that are trending or
adhere to a specific style
Deal of the Day and Bestsellers - It shows the most
attractive offer for a particular day with an easy Buy
Now button that saves the hassle of searching on the
app

Pain points

User is unable to judge how the product actually feels and
looks in reality since he/she cannot know what experience
other previous buyers have had
Designer's Note can seem vague especially given that views
about clothing can be very subjective and it has no
credibility in it
Limited options in Deal of the Day offers, also it is less often
updated giving only few choices to consumers
Limited amount of filters available per product category
which increases scrolling time for user

3) Eat.fit

Interesting Features

Individual Order - Let's you browse through the wide variety of

food/diet available showing their calories and ingredients, with

option to order online for immediate consumption or schedule

for meal of the day

Meal Plan - Offers a weekly curated common menu on

subscription basis and delivers at the planned meal time

Dietician - Consultation with dietician and preparing a curated

personalized menu based on lifestyle and diet requirement

Order                          Meal Plan                   Dietican

Pain Points

Immediate delivery of food, doesn't provide estimated delivery
time till payment. Hence customer won't order for immediate
consumption when hungry without knowing delivery time
No option to search from menu, hence finding a known item
from menu becomes complex as one has to scroll and find
Food Category displayed on top is difficult to navigate when
scrolling, having a category pop-up at bottom right corner will
enable easy navigation
Meal plans are fixed, one can't curate their own meal plan and
place an order for a week entirely. Enabling flexible meal plan
will enable more customers to choose their own meal

4) Wellness
Interesting Features

Doctor Consult - Online consultation with a wide variety of
doctors ranging from COVID care, general physician to
pulmonologist with their experience, availability and fees
Therapy - Therapy sessions for various mental health issues like
anxiety, sleep, stress, depression etc.
Lab Tests - Fulfilled by at-home health test provider Healthians
and enabling a wide range of health checkups at the
convenience of home
Whole.fit Supplements - A division enabling purchase of health
supplements for dietary needs including protein, snacks,
staples etc.
Recipes - A wide variety of healthy home cooked recipes for easy
cooking with health details and step by step procedure

Pain Points

Lack of review or feedback mechanism for Doctors. Lab reports
and supplements provides less credibility on the
services/product provided
Most of the time the recipes option is used when we want to
cook something immediately. Lack of option to choose a recipe
from available ingredients makes it tedious to find the right
recipe

Solutions

Cure.fit

 Energy meter scores can be announced using
motivational statements during the workout so that
users can act upon it
Recommendations for upcoming workouts based on
the fitness journey of the user. This will enable users
to try out newer workouts and gain useful insights
from fitness journey feature
Introduce expert.fit which is an AI Chatbot which
helps users navigate throughout the app and
choose the most appropriate workout without
having to search much for it

Cult.fit store

Add Reviews section for users to post photos and
write product reviews
Sub-categorize sports wear and accessories into
workout intensity to accelerate user's purchase
decision
Add new filters like fabric, fit, body type, price range
in the product page. Add sorting functionality based
on price, popularity, relevance, etc

Solutions

eat.fit:

Add estimated delivery time feature based on
cooking time, proximity of the kitchen and delivery
point
Add search feature to search the required item and
update in-stock status dynamically
Remove horizontal category tab and add pop-up in
bottom right corner for easy navigation in the menu
Enable customizable weekly menu with varying price
to alter existing diet supply

Wellness

Add customer review option to boost engagement
and add credibility to services and products
Add ingredient search/match feature in Recipe

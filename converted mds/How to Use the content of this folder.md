How to Use the content of this folder

Dear PM Enthusiasts,

This document gives a brief of how you can use the resources in this folder

The content for this year comprises of a concept book, casebook, solved interview experiences and
Teardown folder.

We request you to follow the following sequence for your preparation:

1.  Start with the concept book if you are new to PM - Sigma_PM_ConceptBook_PartA_2023
2.  Then solve the cases from the casebook which has questions asked in the interviews and

some samples cases for practice - Sigma PM Casebook (2023-2024)_PartB

3.  You can also mix and match part B with the interview experiences in part c which is the

interview experience book of 2023 , you should not miss out on reading this resource as it
will tell you a lot about the D day - Sigma PM Interview Experiences (2023-24)_PartC
4.  There are some teardowns at your disposal which can support your application knowledge -

Teardown_PartD

5.  Apart from this there are multiple folders which have past experiences and PM books to

prepare you should focus on them for practice and learning the concepts

Team Sigma Wishes you All the Best for your Summer Internship Preparation!!

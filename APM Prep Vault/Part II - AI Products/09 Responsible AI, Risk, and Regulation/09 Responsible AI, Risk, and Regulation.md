# 09 Responsible AI, Risk, and Regulation

AI products can discriminate, leak personal data, produce harmful content, and mislead users, and the rules around them are changing quickly. For a PM, responsible AI is a product requirement: it is designed in, measured, and disclosed, not added after a complaint. This chapter covers bias and representation, privacy under India's DPDP Act 2023, safety and content policy, explainability and disclosure, and the regulatory landscape including the EU AI Act.

## Subsections
- [[9.1 Bias, fairness, and representation]] - where bias enters the data and the product, and how to measure and mitigate it.
- [[9.2 Privacy, consent, and data retention (India DPDP Act 2023)]] - consent, notice, purpose limitation, and retention under the DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules 2025, with the phased timeline to May 2027.
- [[9.3 Safety, misuse, and content policy]] - preventing harmful use, defining what the product will not do, and enforcing it.
- [[9.4 Explainability and disclosure]] - telling users when AI is involved and what it can and cannot do.
- [[9.5 Regulatory landscape - EU AI Act and sector-specific rules]] - EU AI Act tiers and timeline (including the 2026 delay of high-risk duties), India's IT Rules on synthetic content and RBI guidance, and sector rules.

## Key takeaways
- Responsible AI is a set of product requirements with owners, metrics, and launch gates, not a document written once.
- Bias can enter through the data, the labels, the objective, and the evaluation set. Measure performance by segment, not only overall.
- Under India's DPDP Act 2023, personal data processing needs notice and consent (or a listed legitimate use), must be limited to a stated purpose, and must be erased when no longer needed. Most duties apply from 13 May 2027 under the DPDP Rules 2025 (as of November 2026).
- Safety requires a written content policy, technical enforcement, and a way for users to report problems.
- The EU AI Act classifies systems by risk. Prohibitions (Feb 2025) and GPAI duties (Aug 2025) already apply; high-risk duties were delayed to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028. Know your tier before you design.

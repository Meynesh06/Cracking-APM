# 6.3 Pricing models - per seat, usage-based, outcome-based, bundled
> Pick the pricing metric that moves with the value the customer gets and with your cost, so revenue and margin grow together.

## Concept
The Sigma casebook framework lists the common non-hardware pricing options as ad-based, periodic subscription, and freemium-style tiers. AI products add two more metrics that are tied to cost:

| Model | Charge based on | Good when | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per seat | Users per month | Value scales with headcount; predictable revenue | Heavy users cost more than seat price; light users underpay |
| Usage-based | Tokens, requests, documents, minutes | Cost tracks usage closely | Hard for buyers to budget; churn when bills spike |
| Outcome-based | Successful task or resolution | Outcome is measurable and agreed | Needs trustworthy attribution; revenue volatile |
| Credits | Prepaid credits spent on different AI actions | Many features with different costs | Opaque to buyers; credit inflation |
| Bundled | Included in a higher plan or suite | Drives upgrades; raises perceived value | Hides AI cost; hard to price-discriminate |
| Hybrid | Seat fee + usage credits | Most enterprise AI deals | More complex to explain |

Guidelines:
- Price on the metric closest to value and cost (e.g., resolution, document, or seat).
- Keep a free or low-cost tier only if its cost per user is bounded (rate limits, smaller model).
- Include a usage allowance inside the seat price to avoid bill shock; charge overage above it.
- Check the Sigma pricing step: a product that is bundled may still need a stated price anchor for the interview answer.

## Real-world examples
- **GitHub Copilot** - a free tier (from December 2024), individual and business per-seat plans, and monthly "premium request" allowances with paid overage for advanced models (per-seat plus usage).
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot** - launched for enterprises in 2023 as a $30 per user per month add-on (seat-based add-on). In January 2025 Microsoft folded Copilot into consumer Microsoft 365 Personal and Family plans and raised their prices, the bundling move. Google did the same with Gemini in Workspace business plans the same month.
- **Intercom Fin** - priced per resolution (launched at $0.99 per resolution), the outcome-based model.
- **Salesforce Agentforce** - launched in 2024 at $2 per conversation, then added credit-based and per-user options in 2025, showing how vendors iterate on AI pricing metrics as buyers ask for predictability.
- **Spotify (from the July 2022 teardown)** - Free (ad-supported) and Premium (ad-free, offline) tiers, a freemium plus subscription structure that AI features could be bundled into.
- **Sportskeeda (Feb 2023 teardown)** - primarily advertising-funded, with premium and subscription sections added to diversify revenue; a case of ad revenue being a weak base for a new feature.

## Practice questions
1. You are launching an AI meeting-notes feature in a collaboration tool. Seat, usage, or bundle? Justify.
   - Outline: bundle into the paid tier to drive upgrades, with a usage cap for transcription minutes; add a paid add-on for heavy use.
2. A customer says your per-resolution price is unpredictable. How do you respond?
   - Outline: offer a committed monthly bundle with resolution credits and an overage rate; show the cost-per-resolution trend.
3. When is free the right price for an AI feature?
   - Outline: when cost per user is small and bounded (small model, caps), when the feature drives acquisition, retention, or upgrades worth more than its cost, or when competitors give it away. Cap usage and keep the expensive capability for paid tiers.
4. How do you decide between an add-on price and folding AI into the base plan?
   - Outline: add-on when value is concentrated in some users, cost per user is high, and you need to prove willingness to pay; fold in when adoption is the goal, value is broad, and cost per user is low enough to absorb, usually with a base-price increase.

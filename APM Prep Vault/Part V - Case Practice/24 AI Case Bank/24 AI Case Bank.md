# 24 AI Case Bank

Eight full AI case solutions, each walked through clarify, users, pain points, solutions, prioritisation, metrics with guardrails, risks, and a summary. Use them after you have the AI concepts from Part II and the frameworks from Part III, and practise each case from its prompt before reading the model answer.

## Subsections
- [[24.1 Design an AI study assistant for competitive exam aspirants]] - a doubt solver and mistake-driven quiz for exam prep, with DPDP guardrails for minors.
- [[24.2 Add generative AI to a navigation app]] - precomputed review summaries and conversational search first, with in-drive help kept voice-only and hands-free.
- [[24.3 Define success metrics for an AI resume reviewer]] - North Star on interview callbacks, with fabrication and bias as zero-tolerance guardrails.
- [[24.4 Diagnose a rise in chatbot hallucination complaints]] - verify, segment, correlate, reproduce, then fix; includes a cause-signature table.
- [[24.5 Launch an AI feature in a payments app - pricing and risks]] - a merchant dispute assistant: free alerts plus a success fee on won disputes (as Stripe and Chargeflow price), with every claim backed by a real document.
- [[24.6 Build a support copilot for a logistics company]] - agent-first copilot grounded in policy and live data, with actions behind confirmation.
- [[24.7 Should a food delivery app add an AI meal planner]] - a conditional yes: dinner suggestions as a pilot before a full weekly planner.
- [[24.8 An AI feature is cannibalizing the core product]] - decide whether the shift lowers combined margin, measure with a holdout, then hand off to the paid core at high-value moments.

## Key takeaways
- Scope every AI case to one user and one job before proposing features.
- Ground answers in trusted sources and treat fabrication as a zero-tolerance guardrail.
- Put the North Star on the outcome the user wants, not on usage or clicks.
- Keep a human in the loop for consequential actions, and expand autonomy with data.
- Start with low-risk, high-frequency features, and use kill switches and staged rollouts for launch.

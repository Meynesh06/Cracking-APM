# 25 Practice Protocol

How to turn the case material into interview performance: a timed solo drill, peer mocks scored on a shared rubric, and a recording-and-review routine that tracks progress across weeks. The checklist and resource guide set what to cover and in what order.

## Subsections
- [[25.1 Timed solo drill (20 minutes)]] - a minute-by-minute 20-minute drill adapted per case type, plus a 10-minute variant and a follow-up drill.
- [[25.2 Peer mock interviews and a feedback rubric]] - the three-role mock format and an 8-criterion rubric scored 1-5.
- [[25.3 Recording and reviewing your own answers]] - record, review within 24 hours, keep an error log, and re-attempt after a week.

## Key takeaways
- Use a fixed clock: a timed drill teaches you to cover every phase, not just the ones you like.
- Score every mock with the same rubric so you can see progress over weeks.
- Practise the lowest-scoring criterion on purpose, rather than the case you enjoy most.
- Record and review within 24 hours; memory of your reasoning fades fast.
- Re-attempt a case a week later; improvement on a repeat matters more than a good first try.

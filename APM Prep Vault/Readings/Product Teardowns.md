# Product Teardowns

Teardowns to study for product critique and improvement cases, plus local Sigma PM & Tech Club teardowns.

## How to read a teardown
- Identify the user and the job-to-be-done before looking at any screen.
- Note the core loop and the one metric it is probably optimizing.
- Mark each design choice and ask what trade-off it makes (growth vs trust, speed vs control).
- Find one thing you would change, and say what metric it moves and what could go wrong.
- Compare with a competitor or an earlier version to see what is actually distinctive.

Pairs with [[13.1 Five-step critique - JTBD, personas, familiarity, inclusivity, zoom levels]] and [[22.2 Product improvement cases]].

## Teardown hubs
- [Growth.Design case studies](https://www.growth.design/case-studies): long-form UX and psychology breakdowns of well-known products.
- [Built for Mars case studies](https://builtformars.com/case-studies/): detailed UX critiques with concrete fixes.
- [Good UX](https://goodux.appcues.com/) by Appcues: short examples of good onboarding and in-product patterns.
- [Mobbin](https://www.mobbin.com/): a UI pattern library to see how many apps solve the same screen.
- [Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/): recorded user flows for studying onboarding, checkout and upgrade journeys.

## Growth.Design
- [Duolingo user retention](https://growth.design/case-studies/duolingo-user-retention) by Growth.Design: a clear case on habit loops and streaks.
- [Amazon purchase UX](https://growth.design/case-studies/amazon-purchase-ux) by Growth.Design: how friction is removed from checkout.
- [Adobe cancel subscription](https://growth.design/case-studies/adobe-cancel-subscription) by Growth.Design: a study in retention flows and dark-pattern trade-offs.
- [Instagram monetization](https://growth.design/case-studies/instagram-monetization) by Growth.Design: how ads and commerce fit into a social product.
- [Blinkist user onboarding](https://growth.design/case-studies/blinkist-user-onboarding) by Growth.Design: onboarding design that sets expectations and drives activation.

Pairs with [[15.2 AARRR (pirate metrics)]] and [[17.2 Monetization models - subscription, freemium, ads, take rate, razor-and-blade, bundling]].

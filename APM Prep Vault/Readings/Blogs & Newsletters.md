# Blogs & Newsletters

Newsletters, blogs and essays worth following while preparing for APM interviews.

## Newsletters and blogs to follow
- [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/) by Lenny Rachitsky: the most useful single feed for product, growth and career advice.
- [Shreyas Doshi on Substack](https://shreyas.substack.com/) by Shreyas Doshi: sharp, short thinking on prioritization, product sense and PM craft.
- [Shreyas Doshi's site](https://shreyasdoshi.com/) by Shreyas Doshi: collected writing and frameworks in one place.
- [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/) by Ben Thompson: the best source for tech strategy and business-model analysis.
- [Reforge Blog](https://www.reforge.com/blog): practical growth, retention and product strategy writing.
- [Andrew Chen](https://andrewchen.com/) by Andrew Chen: essays on network effects, growth loops and marketplaces.
- [Mind the Product](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/): broad community coverage of product management practice.
- [SVPG](https://www.svpg.com/) by Marty Cagan: the canonical voice on how strong product teams work.
- [Product Growth](https://www.productgrowth.blog/) by Aakash Gupta: interview prep, frameworks and PM career guides.
- [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/): in-depth Indian business and tech stories, useful for India-specific cases.
- [Razorpay Blog](https://razorpay.com/blog/): fintech and payments context for Indian products.

## Product sense and design
- [How to develop product sense](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/product-sense) by Jules Walter: breaks product sense into learnable habits.
- [The definitive guide to mastering product sense interviews](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-definitive-guide-to-mastering) by Lenny's Newsletter: a walkthrough of what interviewers look for.
- [Master the product sense interview](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/master-the-product-sense-interview) by Aakash Gupta: a step-by-step answer structure with examples.
- [10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) by Jakob Nielsen: the standard vocabulary for critiquing any UI.

Pairs with [[12.1 CIRCLES - Comprehend, Identify, Report, Cut, List, Evaluate, Summarize]] and [[13.1 Five-step critique - JTBD, personas, familiarity, inclusivity, zoom levels]].

## Metrics
- [What is good retention?](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-good-retention-issue-29) by Lenny Rachitsky: benchmarks and how to read retention curves.
- [The investor metrics deck](https://andrewchen.com/investor-metrics-deck/) by Andrew Chen: shows which metrics investors actually care about and why.

Pairs with [[15.1 North Star metric]], [[15.4 Funnel, marketplace, and retention metrics]] and [[15.6 Metric trees and diagnosing metric movement]].

## Prioritization
- [RICE: Simple prioritization for product managers](https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/) by Sean McBride: the origin of RICE, straight from Intercom.
- [The LNO framework for product managers](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/lno-framework-for-product-managers) by Aakash Gupta: a lightweight way to decide where to spend effort.

Pairs with [[14.1 RICE]] and [[14.6 Choosing and defending a framework]].

## Strategy
- [Aggregation Theory](https://stratechery.com/2015/aggregation-theory/) by Ben Thompson: explains why platforms win when distribution is free.
- [Getting better at product strategy](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/getting-better-at-product-strategy) by Lenny's Newsletter: practical ways to build a strategy muscle.
- [How to know if you've got product-market fit](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-know-if-youve-got-productmarket) by Lenny's Newsletter: concrete signals and survey methods.
- [Growth inflections](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/growth-inflections) by Lenny's Newsletter: what drives sudden growth shifts in real companies.
- [What's your viral loop?](https://andrewchen.com/whats-your-viral-loop-understanding-the-engine-of-adoption/) by Andrew Chen: a clear model of how products spread.
- [Growth is a system, not a bag of tricks](https://andrewchen.com/growth-is-a-system-not-a-bag-of-tricks/) by Andrew Chen: a useful antidote to growth-hack thinking.

Pairs with [[19.3 Competitive analysis and moats]] and [[19.4 Market sizing and entry decisions]].

## Pricing
- [The art and science of pricing](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-art-and-science-of-pricing-madhavan) by Madhavan Ramanujam: a pricing expert on value-based pricing and willingness to pay.

Pairs with [[17.1 Cost-plus, value-based, and competitive pricing]] and [[17.3 Running pricing experiments]].

## Execution
- [Product vs. feature teams](https://www.svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams/) by Marty Cagan: the difference between teams that own outcomes and teams that ship requests.
- [Product discovery](https://www.svpg.com/product-discovery/) by Marty Cagan: how to test ideas before building them.
- [Inspired: How to create products customers love](https://www.svpg.com/inspired-how-to-create-products-customers-love/) by Marty Cagan: the book page for the PM classic.

Pairs with [[20.1 Theme-based roadmaps and OKRs]] and [[20.2 PRD structure]].

## APM careers
- [How to become a product manager](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/become-a-product-manager) by Lenny's Newsletter: routes into PM and how to stand out.
